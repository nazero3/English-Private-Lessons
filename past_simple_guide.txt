================================================================================
                           THE PAST SIMPLE TENSE
================================================================================

1. WHEN TO USE IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use the Past Simple for actions that started and finished at a specific time in the past.

Timeline:
       PAST                         PRESENT                     FUTURE
  [ Action Done ] -------------------- x --------------------------->
 (e.g., "I visited Paris")           (Now)


2. REGULAR VERBS (-ED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Add "-ed" to the base form of regular verbs:

  * play   --->  played
  * walk   --->  walked
  * watch  --->  watched

Spelling Rules:
  * Verb ends in -e           ---> Add -d          (live -> lived, dance -> danced)
  * Verb ends in Consonant+-y ---> Change -y to -ied (study -> studied, try -> tried)
  * 1-syllable CVC verb       ---> Double final + -ed (stop -> stopped, plan -> planned)


3. COMMON IRREGULAR VERBS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Irregular verbs do not use "-ed". You must memorize their past forms:

  Base Form     Past Simple     Example
  ---------     -----------     ----------------------------------------
  go            went            She went to school yesterday.
  have          had             We had lunch at 1 PM.
  see           saw             I saw a movie last night.
  buy           bought          He bought a new bike.
  make          made            They made a cake.
  eat           ate             We ate pizza for dinner.


4. SENTENCE STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Affirmative (+) : Subject + Verb (Past / V2)
    Example: She visited her friends.

  * Negative (-)    : Subject + did not (didn't) + Verb (Base Form / V1)
    Example: She didn't visit her friends.

  * Question (?)    : Did + Subject + Verb (Base Form / V1)?
    Example: Did she visit her friends?

  [!] GOLDEN RULE:
  When using "did" or "didn't", ALWAYS use the base verb (V1).
  - Incorrect: She didn't went home.
  - Correct  : She didn't go home.


5. THE SPECIAL VERB: TO BE (WAS / WERE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"To be" does NOT use did/didn't in the past:

  * I / He / She / It   ---> was / wasn't
  * You / We / They     ---> were / weren't

  Examples:
  - (+) They were happy yesterday.
  - (-) He wasn't at home last night.
  - (?) Were you tired after work?


6. COMMON TIME EXPRESSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Yesterday (yesterday morning, yesterday evening)
  * Last... (last night, last week, last month, last year)
  * ... ago (two hours ago, three days ago, five years ago)
  * In... (in 2020, in July)
