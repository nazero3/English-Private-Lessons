1) Lesson Objectives

Language Goals
  • Student distinguishes routine vs action now.
  • Student produces 6 correct example sentences.
  • Student speaks using sentence frames with confidence.
Success Criteria
  • 80%+ exercise accuracy.
  • At least 2 self-corrections after feedback.
  • Quiz score 7/10 or higher.

2) Lesson Flow (Private Session)

Time Stage Tutor Actions Student Actions
5-7 min Warm-up Ask 3 easy questions with visuals and Arabic bridge words. Answers with key words or short sentences.
12-15 min Teach Explain rule with two-column visual (habit vs now). Repeats and creates 2 own examples.
12-15 min Guided practice Run gap-fill and correction tasks. Completes exercises and checks errors.
10 min Speaking Role-play with sentence stems. Describes daily routine and current actions.
8-10 min Quiz + Wrap-up Give 10-item mini quiz and review mistakes. Completes quiz and writes correction notes.

3) Visual Explanation Board

Habit (Present Simple): "She studies every day." | Now (Present Progressive): "She is studying now."

4) End-of-Lesson Quiz (Quick Check)

Question Student Correct
1) He ____ to school every day. (go/goes/is going) _____ goes
2) Look! They ____ the room now. (clean/are cleaning/cleans) _____ are cleaning
3) Write one sentence about your daily routine. ______________________________________________

5) Homework and Parent Note

Homework
  • 10 grammar items (mixed).
  • Write 6 sentences (3 routine, 3 now).
  • Record 1-minute speaking practice.
